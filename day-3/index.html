Ada Wong

Ada Wong is one of the most enigmatic and captivating characters in the *Resident Evil* franchise, debuting as a mysterious corporate spy in *Resident Evil 2*.
Clad in her iconic red attire, she operates in the shadows as a brilliant anti-heroine, frequently shifting allegiances between rival bio-weapon corporations while quietly executing her own hidden agendas.
Her cold, calculated professionalism is balanced by her complex, enduring relationship with protagonist Leon S. Kennedy, whom she frequently rescues from dangerous situations despite claiming to only care about her mission.
Beyond her sleek aesthetic and signature grapple gun, Ada is a lethal combatant and master of stealth, effortlessly navigating zombie outbreaks and global bioterror threats with absolute poise.
Ultimately, her true loyalties remain a well-guarded secret, solidifying her status as gaming's ultimate femme fatale.

Leon S.kennedy

Leon S. Kennedy is one of the most iconic and enduring protagonists of the *Resident Evil* series, famously introduced in *Resident Evil 2* as a idealistic rookie police officer surviving his disastrous first day in Raccoon City.
His harrowing escape transforms him from a naive cop into a battle-hardened, elite federal agent, leading to his legendary mission in *Resident Evil 4* to rescue the U.S. President's daughter from a terrifying cult.
Instantly recognizable by his signature curtain hairstyle and tactical gear, Leon balances a gritty, lethal combat skill set with a dry wit, frequently dropping classic one-liners in the face of absolute horror.
His character arc is defined by a deep sense of justice and an unyielding drive to protect the innocent, though he is constantly burdened by the trauma of the bioweapon outbreaks he fights.
This solemn duty is further complicated by his ongoing, cat-and-mouse dynamic with corporate spy Ada Wong, whose paths cross with his across multiple global crises.
Surviving everything from zombie hordes to parasitic mutations, Leon stands as the ultimate resilient protector of the franchise.

Chris Redfield

Chris is the ultimate military powerhouse of the series, starting as a S.T.A.R.S. marksman in the original game and evolving into a legendary tactical commander for the BSAA (Bioterrorism Security Assessment Alliance).
Known for his immense physical strength and unwavering loyalty to his squad, he has spent his entire adult life on a personal crusade to eradicate bioweapons across the globe.
