Resident Evil





Resident Evil 2 is a survival horror game developed and published by Capcom.
The story takes place in the zombie-infested city of Raccoon City.
Players follow rookie police officer Leon S. Kennedy and college student Claire Redfield as they struggle to survive.
The game features intense combat, challenging puzzles, and a dark atmosphere.
Players explore locations such as the Raccoon City Police Department and underground laboratories.
Dangerous enemies, including zombies and bio-organic weapons, constantly threaten the survivors.
The mysterious Umbrella Corporation is responsible for the outbreak.
The game is known for its immersive storytelling, realistic graphics, and suspenseful gameplay.
Resident Evil 2 is widely regarded as one of the greatest survival horror games ever made.

Character Showcase

Leon S. kennedy

Leon S. Kennedy is one of the most iconic and enduring protagonists of the *Resident Evil* series, famously introduced in *Resident Evil 2* as a idealistic rookie police officer surviving his disastrous first day in Raccoon City.
His harrowing escape transforms him from a naive cop into a battle-hardened, elite federal agent, leading to his legendary mission in *Resident Evil 4* to rescue the U.S. President's daughter from a terrifying cult.
Instantly recognizable by his signature curtain hairstyle and tactical gear, Leon balances a gritty, lethal combat skill set with a dry wit, frequently dropping classic one-liners in the face of absolute horror.
His character arc is defined by a deep sense of justice and an unyielding drive to protect the innocent, though he is constantly burdened by the trauma of the bioweapon outbreaks he fights.
This solemn duty is further complicated by his ongoing, cat-and-mouse dynamic with corporate spy Ada Wong, whose paths cross with his across multiple global crises.
Surviving everything from zombie hordes to parasitic mutations, Leon stands as the ultimate resilient protector of the franchise.

Claire Redfield
Claire Redfield is a brave college student who comes to Raccoon City in search of her missing brother, Chris Redfield.
During the outbreak, she faces countless dangers while trying to survive. Claire takes responsibility for protecting Sherry Birkin, a young girl caught in the chaos.
Her determination, compassion, and resourcefulness help her overcome many challenges.
She eventually uncovers important secrets about the Umbrella Corporation and becomes one of the series' most respected characters.

Ada Wong
Ada Wong is a mysterious and intelligent woman who becomes involved in the events of Raccoon City.
She is skilled in espionage, combat, and investigation, often hiding her true motives from others.
Throughout the story, Ada assists Leon several times while carrying out her own secret mission.
Her calm personality and unpredictable actions make her one of the most intriguing characters in the game.
Despite her mysterious nature, she plays an important role in Leon's survival.

Sherry Birkin
Sherry Birkin is a young girl who becomes trapped in Raccoon City during the outbreak. As the daughter of William and Annette Birkin,
she is closely connected to the dangerous G-virus. Claire Redfield finds and protects her throughout the story, forming a strong bond with her.
Despite facing terrifying situations, Sherry remains brave and hopeful. Her survival becomes one of the central goals of Claire's journey.

Mr.X
Mr. X is a powerful Tyrant bio-weapon created to eliminate survivors and cover up evidence of the outbreak.
He relentlessly hunts the player through the Raccoon City Police Department, creating a constant sense of fear and tension.
With immense strength and durability, he can withstand heavy damage and continue his pursuit.
His intimidating appearance and unstoppable nature make him one of the most memorable enemies in survival horror gaming.
Mr. X is widely recognized as a symbol of Resident Evil 2's horror experience.

William Birkin
William Birkin is a brilliant scientist responsible for creating the powerful G-virus.
After being betrayed by Umbrella operatives, he injects himself with the virus in an attempt to survive.
This decision transforms him into a horrifying mutant that becomes increasingly dangerous as the story progresses.
His mutations lead to several intense boss battles throughout the game.
William Birkin's actions are a major cause of the Raccoon City outbreak and the events of Resident Evil 2.

Story Timeline

Arrival At Raccon City
Leon S. Kennedy arrives in Raccoon City on his first day as a police officer, while Claire Redfield arrives searching for her brother Chris.
Both are unaware that the city has been devastated by a deadly virus outbreak.
After meeting at a gas station overrun by zombies, they escape together but become separated after a truck crash.

Journey To The R.P.D
After the crash, Leon and Claire make their way through the zombie-infested streets toward the Raccoon City Police Department (R.P.D.), hoping to find safety and survivors.
Along the way, they encounter numerous infected citizens and witness the scale of the disaster.
The city is in complete chaos, with abandoned vehicles, burning buildings, and roaming monsters everywhere.

Exploring The Police Station
Inside the R.P.D., Leon and Claire discover that most officers have been killed or infected.
They search the building for survivors, keys, weapons, and clues about the outbreak.
The station is filled with puzzles, locked rooms, and terrifying enemies. Here, Leon meets Ada Wong while Claire encounters Sherry Birkin.

The Tyrant Appears
The bio-weapon known as Mr. X is deployed into the police station. Unlike ordinary enemies, he relentlessly hunts Leon and Claire throughout the building.
His heavy footsteps and sudden appearances create constant tension and force the player to stay alert.

Escape Through the Sewers
After uncovering important information inside the R.P.D., the protagonists escape into the underground sewer system.
The sewers are filled with dangerous creatures, hidden laboratories, and evidence of illegal biological experiments.
They learn more about the mysterious G-virus and the role of the Umbrella Corporation.

William Birkin's Mutation
The creator of the G-virus, mutates into a horrifying monster after injecting himself with his own creation.
Throughout the story, he continues to evolve into stronger and more dangerous forms. Leon and Claire must confront him multiple times to survive

Umbrella's Secret Laboratory
The journey leads to NEST, Umbrella Corporation's secret underground research facility. Here, the truth behind the outbreak is revealed.
The laboratory contains advanced technology, dangerous experiments, and records exposing Umbrella's involvement in the catastrophe.

Final Battle and Escape
Leon and Claire face the final forms of William Birkin in intense battles.
They activate the facility's self-destruct sequence and race against time to escape before the laboratory is destroyed.
The fight tests all their skills and determination.

The Survivors Leave Raccoon City
After defeating the monsters and escaping the laboratory, Leon, Claire, and Sherry Birkin leave the city alive.
Although they survive, they know the nightmare is far from over. Their experiences in Raccoon City shape their future roles in the Resident Evil series.
